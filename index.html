Every week of a life, as a grid. Periods are chapters; milestones are moments.

You

The default 79 is US life expectancy at birth, 2024 (all sexes, NCHS). Edit it for another country or table — note the sex adjustment below uses the US gaps and is calibrated to that US baseline.
Deltas are approximate pooled estimates from large cohort studies and meta-analyses. Two overlapping pairs are capped rather than summed: exercise + strength (max +3 yrs) and social ties + partnership (max +2 yrs). A few — income, education — are associations, not proven causes, and every one carries wide uncertainty. A teaching model, not a prediction about you. Toggle the factors and watch the grid breathe.
The grid runs from birth to this age. Set it from family history, actuarial tables, or a goal.
